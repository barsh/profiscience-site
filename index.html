CLESite

10 years of continuous improvement, shaped by law-firm CLE administrators.

Since its introduction in 2016, CLESite has been adopted by more than half of Profiscience’s law-firm clients. It supports the distinct needs of CLE administrators, attorneys, and firm leadership.

For CLE administrators, CLESite reduces manual work by bringing records, certificates, deadlines, provider reporting, and jurisdiction-specific requirements into one system.

For attorneys, it provides a centralized place to review CLE obligations, learning activity, and supporting documentation.

For firm leadership, it offers clearer visibility into renewal deadlines, compliance progress, and areas that may require attention.
