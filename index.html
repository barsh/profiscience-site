Why do learning programs become difficult to manage as firms grow?

As firms grow, learning becomes harder to coordinate across offices, audiences, programs, content, assignments, attendance, and reporting. Processes that once worked for a smaller firm often become fragmented, manual, and difficult to oversee.

UniversitySite brings that work into one flexible platform for onboarding, professional development, technology training, security awareness, compliance programs, and firm-wide learning.

Why are firm-wide CLE obligations so difficult to manage?

CLE becomes difficult to oversee when records, certificates, provider activity, reporting deadlines, and jurisdiction-specific requirements must be coordinated across attorneys, administrative teams, offices, and jurisdictions. Many firms are still relying on long-standing CLE systems that have not kept pace with the complexity of that work.

CLESite helps administrators manage records, requirements, and reporting, gives attorneys a clearer view of what they have completed and what remains, and provides firm leadership with more reliable oversight.

Over the past 10 years, CLESite has evolved through weekly updates shaped by feedback from law-firm CLE administrators.
