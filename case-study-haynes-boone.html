Haynes Boone integrates UniversitySite into their employee portal.

Training compliance was invisible, so it slipped. The fix was to put it where people already look.

View the PDF Let's Talk

Overview

Haynes Boone saw a need to alert employees of required training and upcoming training deadlines. They decided the best way to do this was to show alerts in their employee portal.

“Historically, we have struggled with compliance for mandatory employee training. As we considered the root causes, we realized that compliance status was not adequately visible. It became clear that surfacing information in the right places and making it easy for leadership to look up compliance status would be beneficial. This meant incorporating UniversitySite into our employee portal.”
KG
Kurt Geisler CTO, Haynes Boone

Integration with the employee portal

Haynes Boone's internal employee portal is one of the best law firm intranets we have ever seen. Sandro Juric, Manager of Application Development at Haynes Boone, said, “We use UniversitySite's API to show alerts to people who haven't completed their required training.”

When employees open their web browser, they see the employee portal — and if they have required training outstanding, they see a notification.

The product

Haynes Boone used UniversitySite's Software Developer Kit (SDK) to integrate their employee portal. The SDK Extension provides access to UniversitySite cloud-based data in real time. It comprises a REST-based API that returns data in JSON format, which can be integrated into other programs.

“Clients are asking us if we have a 100% success rate with security awareness training. It's very important to us as we're getting audited constantly by clients now. Our employee portal integration with UniversitySite has been very helpful.”
KG
Kurt Geisler CTO, Haynes Boone
