Company timeline

How has Profiscience evolved alongside law firms?

The platform has expanded as firms’ learning, CLE, compliance, and technology needs have become more connected and more complex.

  1. 2000: A focus on law-firm learning begins

    Profiscience begins developing learning technology specifically for law firms.

  2. 2010-2015: UniversitySite expands globally

    The platform grows to support law-firm learning across the Americas, Australia, and Europe.

  3. 2016: Learning and CLE become more connected

    CLESite is introduced to bring specialized CLE administration and oversight alongside UniversitySite.

  4. 2017-2025: CLESite adoption continues to grow

    More than half of Profiscience clients now use CLESite alongside UniversitySite.

  5. ✦ Now

    Today: The platform continues to improve every week

    Client feedback continues to shape weekly platform releases, including improvements to automation, reporting, integrations, and carefully governed AI capabilities.

Our mission

What should learning technology make easier?

The best learning technology should help firms spend less time managing the process and more time supporting attorney and staff development.

Profiscience brings learning and compliance into a more connected environment, helping law firms reduce fragmented processes without forcing every firm into the same way of working.

The outcomes we focus on

Make learning easier to deliver and access

Support onboarding, professional development, continuing education, and firm knowledge through a more connected experience.

Reduce avoidable administrative work

Bring assignments, records, certificates, communications, and reporting into more coordinated workflows.

Give the firm clearer oversight

Help attorneys, administrators, and leadership understand requirements, deadlines, progress, and potential compliance concerns.
