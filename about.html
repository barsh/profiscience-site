Our mission

Help law firms do more with learning technology.

Profiscience's mission is to help law firms produce better lawyers, win more business, operate more efficiently, and comply with the laws, policies, and regulations that govern them.

We built UniversitySite and CLESite because the tools most law firms were using were too generic, too slow, and too expensive to maintain. Our clients needed a platform that understood the difference between a CLE credit hour and a webinar CE — and handled both without a consultant in the room.
