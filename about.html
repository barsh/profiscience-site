Company timeline

How Profiscience evolved.

  1. 2000: Company founded

    Profiscience begins developing learning technology for law firms.

  2. 2010-2015: UniversitySite expands globally

    UniversitySite grows to support law-learning across the Americas, Australia, and Europe.

  3. 2016: CLESite introduced

    CLESite is introduced, adding specialized CLE administration and oversight.

  4. 2025: CLESite Adoption Milestone

Our mission

Help law firms do more with learning technology.

Our mission is to help law firms manage learning and compliance more effectively—supporting better learning experiences, more manageable administration, and clearer firmwide visibility.

UniversitySite and CLESite were built around the practical realities of legal learning, from onboarding and professional development to CLE administration and compliance oversight. We focus on giving firms tools that fit their work.
