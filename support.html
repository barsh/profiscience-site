FAQ

Common questions.

What's the average support response time?

Under two business hours for Standard support. Enterprise accounts receive 24/7 email and phone coverage with a guaranteed one-hour response SLA.

Do you offer onboarding assistance?

Yes. Every new customer is assigned a named success partner who guides setup, data migration, and initial training sessions for your admin team.

Is there a self-service knowledge base?

Help articles and short video walkthroughs are accessible directly inside UniversitySite via the in-app help panel on every page.

Who do I contact for billing or account changes?

For account-level changes, contact support@profiscience.com or reach out to your account manager directly.

Can I request a custom feature or integration?

Absolutely. Profiscience has a track record of building custom extensions — from Workday integrations to firm-branded portals. Start a conversation via the contact form.

Is support included in my subscription?

Yes. Standard email support is included in all plans. Priority and 24/7 coverage are available on Professional and Enterprise tiers. See the pricing page for a full comparison.

Can't find what you need?

Send us a message and a Profiscience specialist will get back to you within two business hours.
