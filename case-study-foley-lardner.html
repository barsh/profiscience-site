Foley & Lardner brings LinkedIn Learning inside UniversitySite.

A firm-wide enrichment plan for legal assistants, staff, and managers — delivered through one familiar platform, with 90% participation.

View the PDF Let's Talk

Overview

Foley & Lardner LLP set out to supplement their existing learning content with LinkedIn Learning's vast library, to provide power-skills training for technology staff, legal assistants, and managers. The firm also needed the ability to assign learning plans and track completion rates and employee progress. UniversitySite was able to meet this need.

The LinkedIn Learning Extension lets the firm integrate LinkedIn Learning modules with the learning content already in UniversitySite, creating customized learning plans for each group. Once the plans were set up and assigned, the firm could track employee progress and completion rates — and learners reached LinkedIn Learning content through UniversitySite, where they were already accustomed to finding learning resources.

The solution: the LinkedIn Learning Extension

The extension incorporates the single sign-on technology of UniversitySite while leveraging LinkedIn's digital courses inside UniversitySite for technical, power-skills, and professional development. It provides the ability to import LinkedIn Learning courses through the eLearning store in InstructorSite, and LinkedIn courses can be launched directly from UniversitySite.

Foley & Lardner used the extension to implement a legal-assistant enrichment plan, so registrants received the training needed to succeed in their roles. The firm could also take courses from LinkedIn Learning and break them into chapters — choosing specific sections of a course to assign — and build learning plans that send automatic reminders.

The benefits

  • Seamless access to LinkedIn Learning content via single sign-on.
  • Easily import LinkedIn content into the UniversitySite course catalog.
  • LinkedIn Learning courses launch directly from UniversitySite.
  • Automatic reminders, plus tracking for learning-plan progress and course completion rates.
