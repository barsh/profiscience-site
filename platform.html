UniversitySite · Learning Platform

What does UniversitySite provide beyond a traditional LMS?

Traditional learning management systems are designed primarily to deliver training. Law firms also need to coordinate programs, support different audiences, maintain learning records, and give managers appropriate visibility. UniversitySite also supports the operational work of running learning across a law firm, with purpose-built experiences for administrators, learners, managers, and firm leaders.

Use one shared platform to organize onboarding, required training, professional development, continuing education, live programs, on-demand learning, and firmwide learning records—while giving each group the tools and visibility appropriate to its role.

CLESite · Built on UniversitySite

What does it take to keep CLE records, requirements, and reporting under control?

Keeping CLE under control requires more than tracking credits. Firms need dependable attorney records, current jurisdiction requirements, reliable compliance calculations, and clear visibility before deadlines arrive.

CLESite brings those pieces together by building specialized CLE administration on the UniversitySite learning foundation.

CLE RECORDS

Maintain reliable CLE records

Bring internal programs, external activity, certificates, carryover, and historical records into one dependable record for each attorney, reducing the gaps and reconciliation work that make compliance harder to trust.

  • Consolidate CLE activity and certificates
  • Import historical and external records
  • Reduce spreadsheet-based tracking
  • Preserve a complete attorney history
COMPLIANCE OVERSIGHT

Know who is on track—and who needs attention

Apply jurisdiction-specific rules to calculate requirements, deadlines, progress, and compliance status, so administrators can act before missing credits or approaching deadlines become urgent.

  • Calculate progress by jurisdiction
  • Identify missing credits and approaching deadlines
  • Support multiple admissions per attorney
  • Account for carryover and category requirements
ATTORNEY EXPERIENCE

Help attorneys understand what they need to do

Give attorneys one place to review obligations, progress, activity, and certificates, reducing routine questions while making the next required action clearer.

  • Find records and certificates quickly
  • Review progress toward requirements
  • Submit or confirm external activity
  • Prepare for bar reporting and renewal
FIRMWIDE OVERSIGHT

Give leadership a clearer view of compliance risk

See progress across attorneys, offices, practice groups, and jurisdictions, so the firm can identify areas requiring attention without assembling reports from separate sources.

  • See who is on track and who needs attention
  • Report across jurisdictions and offices
  • Manage corrections and supporting documentation
  • Communicate deadlines and required actions

Migration & Implementation

How much of your current environment can move with you?

Most firms evaluating Profiscience already have a learning platform, a CLE tracking system, or both. Every migration is different, so we work with each firm to determine what should be migrated, what should be rebuilt, and what can be simplified.

Depending on the existing environment and available data, migration may include learning history, CLE activity, users, organizational structure, content, assignments, and other information needed to support continuity.

LEARNING HISTORY

Preserve completed learning records

Bring forward relevant completion history, attendance, assignments, and other learning records where the source data supports a reliable migration.

CLE HISTORY

Maintain continuity for CLE oversight

Migrate historical CLE activity, credits, certificates, and related records using an approach suited to the firm's data and compliance requirements.

USERS & STRUCTURE

Recreate the firm accurately

Import users and available organizational information such as offices, departments, practice groups, roles, and reporting relationships.

CONTENT & WORKFLOWS

Carry forward what still serves the firm

Evaluate courses, SCORM packages, videos, resources, assignments, and administrative workflows to determine what should be reused, rebuilt, or retired.

What does staying with your current system cost?

Continuing with an aging or poorly matched system may avoid disruption today, but it can also preserve manual work, limited visibility, and growing dependence on workarounds. Every year spent adapting to the system is another year the system isn't adapting to your firm.

Migration is also an opportunity to improve the process.

A successful migration should do more than move data. It should preserve what matters, simplify unnecessary complexity, and establish a stronger foundation for learning and CLE administration going forward.
