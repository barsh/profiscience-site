UniversitySite · Learning Platform

The foundation for learning across the firm.

UniversitySite provides the shared platform for managing onboarding, required training, professional development, continuing education, and other firm learning programs. It brings together the experiences used to administer learning, participate in programs, and provide appropriate visibility to managers and firm leaders.

Use one platform to organize onboarding, required training, professional development, live programs, on-demand learning, and firm-wide learning records.

Included with UniversitySite

Administration and coordination

InstructorSite

Give learning administrators, instructors, and coordinators the tools to organize programs, manage audiences and requirements, support instructor-led learning, maintain records, and report on participation and progress.

The learner experience

LearningSite

Give attorneys and staff one place to discover programs, complete assignments, access learning resources, and review their learning history.

Manager visibility

ManagerSite

Provide designated managers and leaders with appropriate visibility into the learning activity and progress of the people or groups they oversee, without giving them full administrative access.

CLESite · Powered by UniversitySite

Bring CLE records, obligations, and oversight into one connected environment.

CLESite builds specialized CLE administration on the UniversitySite learning foundation, so learning activity, attorney records, certificates, provider information, and compliance reporting can be managed through connected workflows rather than separate systems.

CLE RECORDS

Maintain reliable CLE records

Bring internal programs, external activity, certificates, carryover, and historical records into one dependable CLE record for each attorney.

  • Consolidate CLE activity and certificates
  • Import historical and external records
  • Reduce spreadsheet-based tracking
  • Preserve a complete attorney history
COMPLIANCE OVERSIGHT

Understand obligations and compliance

Apply jurisdiction-specific rules to calculate requirements, deadlines, progress, and compliance status.

  • Calculate progress by jurisdiction
  • Identify missing credits and approaching deadlines
  • Support multiple admissions per attorney
  • Account for carryover and category requirements
ATTORNEY EXPERIENCE

Give attorneys one place to manage CLE

Provide attorneys with direct access to their learning history, certificates, upcoming requirements, and compliance information.

  • Find records and certificates quickly
  • Review progress toward requirements
  • Submit or confirm external activity
  • Prepare for bar reporting and renewal
FIRMWIDE OVERSIGHT

Give the firm clearer oversight

Help administrators monitor obligations, resolve exceptions, and document compliance across the firm.

  • See who is on track and who needs attention
  • Report across jurisdictions and offices
  • Manage corrections and supporting documentation
  • Communicate deadlines and required actions
