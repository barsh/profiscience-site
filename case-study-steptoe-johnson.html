Overview

COVID-19 caused a change in the way firms manage their employees, including allowing them to work remotely. When firms began opening their offices to employees, the need to ensure the safety of all employees came to the forefront.

The law firm of Steptoe & Johnson created a task force whose mission was to create a safe plan for employees to return to their offices. The firm created a policy that required employees to take a self-assessment each morning before being permitted to come to the office. Steptoe & Johnson needed a way to deliver this assessment to all employees efficiently and effectively.

The solution: a daily self-assessment

UniversitySite provided a solution. All employees of the firm already had access to UniversitySite. The platform allowed office managers to know if an employee was clear to come to the office while preserving medical confidentiality — only select members of management were given full access to results.

The firm published the assessment through UniversitySite. A link was sent to all employees and pushed to their mobile phones, allowing them to quickly comply with the new company policy.

The benefits

Using their existing UniversitySite platform for COVID-19 safety compliance saved Steptoe & Johnson time, money, and resources. UniversitySite was already in place, and it could manage daily self-assessments, so there was no need to spend time and money researching any other system. The lawyers and support staff already knew how to use it.

  • No staff member had to be assigned to track results by hand.
  • No separate tracking system had to be built or bought.
  • No one had to take temperatures at the door each day.
  • Employees completed the assessment with little to no resistance to the process.

Using existing systems that people already knew, the firm could put their time, money, and resources toward other purposes. The experience helped the firm see that there are many unique and creative ways to use UniversitySite to their benefit.
