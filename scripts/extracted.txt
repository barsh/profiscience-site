## Why you won't find a price tag here.
It isn't a game. Publishing a number before we understand your firm would do you a disservice — here's the honest reasoning.

### Because every firm is different
A 40-attorney firm standing up CLE tracking and a 900-person firm consolidating five systems aren't buying the same thing. A single list price would be wrong for almost everyone who read it.

A price with no understanding of your goals, timeline, and rollout just invites a bad comparison. A short conversation first means the number you eventually see is one you can actually act on.

FAQ
## Straight answers about pricing.
Why don't you just publish prices?
Because the honest answer to "what does it cost?" is "what do you need?" We price to the products, audience size, and support level that fit your firm — a list price would be wrong for almost everyone. We'll give you a real number as soon as we understand the shape of the work.
Can I buy just one product?
Yes. Plenty of firms run only UniversitySite, or come to us specifically for CLESite. The platform is modular — you take what solves your problem and nothing you don't.
Do you build custom packages?
That's the norm, not the exception. Most engagements are a specific combination of products, extensions, and service levels assembled around one firm's needs. The "package" is whatever fits you.
Will I get pressured to buy the whole suite?
No. Our approach is the opposite — we'd rather scope you smaller and have you grow into more when it genuinely helps. Adding capabilities later doesn't mean starting over.
What does the first conversation actually involve?
A short, no-obligation call to understand what you're solving for. You'll leave with a clear sense of fit and next steps — and if we're not the right choice, we'll tell you.
