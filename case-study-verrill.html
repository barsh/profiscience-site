Overview

Verrill, a growing New England law firm, has quickly increased their number of staff and attorneys. As a result, they realized a Learning Management System (LMS) was necessary to equip their firm for future growth.

Through an arduous evaluation process they concluded that UniversitySite fit their needs. After implementing it, they feel equipped to accomplish more than they initially envisioned — because of the platform's many features, versatility, and ease of use.

The product

UniversitySite is an excellent tool for establishing learning plans for different groups of users and then measuring individual performance against each plan. It enables a firm to schedule and manage training to support its technology initiatives.

It also allows for building a university-style continuing education program with instructor-led learning and eLearning — which can ultimately lead to creating a culture of learning and accountability.

The benefits

Verrill gained robust reporting features and learning plans for employee development, with training and compliance tracked across the moments that matter most:

  • Onboarding new employees
  • Security awareness
  • Software rollouts
  • Professional development
