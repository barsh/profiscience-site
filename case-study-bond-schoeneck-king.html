Overview

Bond is a reputable law firm with a long history, having been founded in Syracuse, New York over a century ago. Initially known for its expertise in labor and employment law, the firm has since expanded its scope to include 33 practice areas across 11 industry groups, serving clients in five states through 15 offices.

In 2019, the state of New York enacted a law requiring all employers to provide sexual harassment training to their employees. Bond, which had previously offered in-person training to clients, saw the opportunity to expand its reach and created an online platform for the training program.

This resulted in a significant source of revenue for the firm — equivalent to that of a Top 50 client. Beyond the revenue, Bond needed a solution that could accommodate hundreds of clients with multiple learning plans and thousands of users.

Advice from Bond, Schoeneck & King

Asked what advice they would give someone starting a similar journey, Bond recommends seeking training from Profiscience and setting up test scenarios until you feel confident in your understanding of the product. It is also important to keep using the product during the lead-up to going live, to ensure a smooth transition.
